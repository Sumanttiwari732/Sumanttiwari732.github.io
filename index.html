AI classification of seminoma and non-seminoma testicular germ cell tumors on MRI

Seminoma (SGCT) and non-seminoma (NSGCT) are treated differently but can look alike on MRI. I tested whether the tumor and the tissue around it (0–3, 3–5 and 5–7 mm rings, limited to testicular parenchyma) can separate the two noninvasively, comparing three model families on identical patient-level splits.

MRI-CORE foundation model, intratumoral0.911AUC ± 0.062 · P = .001 · n = 23
Radiomics, tumor + peritumor fusion0.848AUC ± 0.087 · P = .008
3–5 mm peritumoral radiomics0.87AUC · n = 36 · vs 0.71 intratumoral
  • Frozen MRI-CORE ViT-B embeddings → in-fold PCA (5 PCs) → class-balanced logistic regression; ResNet50 and radiomics as baselines
  • 1,794 intratumoral and 264 peritumoral radiomic features; Mann–Whitney, effect-size and correlation screening inside training folds; L1 logistic regression
  • Repeated stratified 3-fold × 20-repeat cross-validation; equal-weight late fusion of held-out probabilities to limit overfitting in a small cohort
  • GLSZM Zone Entropy heatmaps across peritumoral rings; exploratory nnU-Net tumor segmentation on T2W + ADC (Dice ≈ 0.90)

Predicting relapse after primary RPLND in low-volume seminoma

Can pre-surgery CT predict which low-volume seminoma patients (largest node ≤ 3 cm) relapse after retroperitoneal lymph node dissection? I annotate the scans and run frozen foundation-model encoders against radiomics and CNN baselines.

  • RadFM and CT-FM embeddings → fold-local PCA → logistic regression, patient-level nested cross-validation
  • Clinical + imaging fusion models; radiomics and CNN as comparators
  • Feature extraction on IU's Quartz HPC cluster with Slurm batch jobs

Multi-center batch effects in head-and-neck tumor segmentation

Trained nnU-Net on the HECKTOR challenge data and showed that radiomic features differ significantly by clinical center, a confound that can inflate reported model performance.

  • ANOVA and Levene's tests confirmed center-level batch effects in PyRadiomics features
  • Center-classifier leakage test: if a model can tell which hospital a scan came from, features are not harmonized
  • Compared ComBat, z-score normalization and histogram matching
